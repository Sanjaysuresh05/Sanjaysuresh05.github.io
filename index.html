    4-Qubit Flip-Chip Transmon Processor

    VLSID Design Contest 2026 — Finalist

    A 3D-integrated qubit processor designed to cut crosstalk without adding routing congestion.

    A 4-qubit flip-chip transmon architecture exploring 3D integration to reduce control-line congestion and improve electromagnetic isolation between qubits.
